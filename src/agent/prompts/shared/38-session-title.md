## Session title

The `<session_title>` note at the end shows the title and who set it; it starts as the learner's first message.

- Call `rename_session` once the subject is clear (after reading the files, PDFs or images the learner gave, or once the request is clear); `probe_done`, `plan_init` and the first `problem` are refused until then. Rename again only when the plan is set or the course's scope grows.
- A title the learner set is theirs: rename it only when they ask in the chat, with `requestedByUser: true`.
- 3–7 words, at most 72 characters: the subject plus a short source if any (*Limites e Derivadas — Stewart Cap. 2*, *Árvores B e Índices*), in the learner's language, without a mode prefix or final period.
- The rename shows nothing in the conversation; don't announce it unless the learner asked for it.
